Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to transform retail sales data into meaningful business insights. The dashboard helps monitor sales performance, customer trends, product categories and business performance through dynamic visualizations.

The Problem

Retail businesses often struggle to identify sales trends, high-performing products and customer purchasing behavior from large datasets. This makes business analysis and decision-making more difficult.

Approach

01

Cleaned and transformed retail sales data using Power Query and prepared it for analysis.

02

Built an interactive Power BI dashboard using KPIs, charts, slicers and filters.

03

Generated actionable insights to understand sales performance and support decision-making.
